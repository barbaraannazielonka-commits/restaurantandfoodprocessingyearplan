English for Specific Purposes: the weekly kitchen session

Every week there is a 45 minute session in the kitchen where I talk with you and assess your vocational performance in English. This is a chance to use English for Specific Purposes in a real vocational setting, and to practice the language, terminology and communication skills you will need at your future workplace. I normally select three students each week for an individual conversation and assessment.

All learning materials, presentations, quizzes, word lists and other resources can be found in OneNote. You are expected to use OneNote actively throughout the year to prepare for lessons, review vocabulary and revise for assessments.
